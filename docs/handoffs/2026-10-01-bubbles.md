# 2026-10-01: speech bubbles, chimes, quiet pushes, project memory

## State
- Sessions keep `message` (last answer, 600 chars, local only) and `since` (state start).
- PC: TerminalBlinkies opens a BlinkyBubble left of the corner blinky on done/waiting/question/error
  and chimes once (`blinky chime`, synthesised WAVs in ~/.cache/blinky, pw-play/paplay/aplay);
  it closes once the terminal had focus after `since` (HyprlandWindows.focusedAt). Click on a
  blinky peeks at the last answer, click on the bubble focuses the terminal.
- Phone: "done" is priority 2 and bundled (held 45 s by a detached `blinky flush`, sent as one
  push; a failed send keeps the items and the next push retries); attention stays 4 and
  immediate. ntfy's notification replacement (sequence id) is Android/web only, so not used.
- Memory: `blinky place on` remembers the projects in that terminal (state dir, survives reboot);
  BlinkySessions auto-places a remembered blinky on the next uncovered terminal of that project;
  `place off` forgets.
- Checked: 30 unit tests (bundling incl. failed send, memory, message/since, chime levels),
  qmllint, offscreen render of bubble + blinky, both chimes played once on this machine.
